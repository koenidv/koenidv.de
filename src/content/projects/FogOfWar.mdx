### Highlights

- **Completely Private**: Built with zero server communication. Your 80+ MB location history is parsed and indexed entirely in a background Web Worker in your browser and never leaves your device.
- **Hardware-Accelerated WebGL2**: Renders hundreds of thousands of GPS fixes, journeys, and visits smoothly via instanced capsule rendering with analytic antialiased SDF coverage unioning.
- **Customizable Aesthetics**: Switch between dark-matter, positron, and voyager basemaps, customize fog density, atmospheric noise, rim glow, and reveal radii, or choose from curated presets.
- **High-Resolution Poster Export**: Generate crisp PNG prints up to 300 DPI (A3, A2, 4K wallpapers) with clean typography and baked attribution.
